Privacy Policy

How this website handles information, cookies, and advertising.

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Awesome Intelligence （AI不够酷） ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the website at awesomeintelligence.top (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

The Site is a static website. We do not operate user accounts, and we do not ask visitors to register or submit personal information in order to read our content. The information involved in your visit is limited to:

  • Log and device data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and version, device type, referring page, and the pages you view with timestamps. This data is used to operate, secure, and understand the usage of the Site.
  • Information you send us. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message, and we use them only to respond to you.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, as described in the next sections.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by your browser. The Site itself sets no first-party cookies for tracking. However, third-party services used on the Site — in particular Google AdSense — may set and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons and similar technologies, as described below. You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings; disabling cookies will not prevent you from reading the Site, though advertising may become less relevant.

Google AdSense and the DoubleClick cookie

We use Google AdSense to display advertising on the Site. In connection with this:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this Site.
  • Google's use of the DoubleClick (DART) cookie enables Google and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Third-party vendors and ad networks, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect or receive information from this Site and elsewhere on the Internet, and use that information to provide measurement services and to target ads.

You may opt out of the use of the DoubleClick cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of personalized advertising from many third-party vendors at once via aboutads.info/choices (or, in the European Economic Area, youronlinechoices.eu). For more detail on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google's partner-sites policy.

Links to other sites

Our articles link to third-party websites, including open-source project repositories. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page, with the "Last updated" date revised accordingly.
