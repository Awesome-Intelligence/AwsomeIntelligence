About Awesome Intelligence

AI不够酷 — "AI isn't cool enough" — is our name and our standard.

Awesome Intelligence is an independent studio working on three things: AI media, AI applications, and agent systems. We build products of our own, and we write about the tools — mostly open source — that move the field forward. Everything on this site is produced by a small team of builders who would rather ship something real than talk about shipping it.

What this blog is

The blog is where we publish hands-on reviews of open-source AI projects and developer tools: the fast-moving repositories on GitHub's trending charts, agent frameworks, coding assistants, and the occasional odd utility that turns out to be genuinely useful. Our approach is simple:

  • We read the repository, not just the launch post. Star counts, release history, architecture, and limitations are checked against the source.
  • We say who a tool is for — and who it is not for. A review that recommends everything recommends nothing.
  • We flag the rough edges. If a project is early alpha, Windows-only, or held together by one maintainer, you will read that here.

The blog is the English edition of our long-running Chinese tech media work, where we have covered open-source AI projects for a developer and product audience since 2024.

What we build

  • Trace — a local-first personal self-management app (goals, habits, records, and relationships, stored as Markdown files you own), for Android, iOS, and WeChat.
  • Agent-Z — an enterprise-grade agent system aimed at agents that can genuinely make decisions and improve over time, not just answer prompts.
  • DayDayUp — a learning app in development, built around annotating AI answers and asking better follow-up questions.

More about our products is on the homepage. If you maintain an open-source project you think we should review, or you want to reach the team for any other reason, head to the contact page.
